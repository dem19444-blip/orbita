ORBITA

Master Your Breath, Optimize Your Life

Orbita bridges the gap between ancient wisdom and modern respiratory science. By training your breathing, you directly influence your nervous system, heart rate variability (HRV), and cellular oxygenation.

The Bohr Effect: Energy isn’t just taking more oxygen in—it’s getting it out of your blood and into your cells. This requires Carbon Dioxide (CO₂). Orbita trains your brain to tolerate CO₂, "unlocking" your blood's ability to release oxygen to your brain and muscles.

Nasal Breathing: Breathing should be exclusively nasal. Your nose filters air while producing Nitric Oxide—a molecule that dilates blood vessels, increases oxygen uptake, and acts as a natural antiviral.

Goals & Benefits

• Recovery: Resonance 5.5 synchronizes heart and lungs to maximize recovery and balance the nervous system.
• Stress Reset: Physiological Sigh and Extended Exhales stimulate the Vagus Nerve to shift you into "Rest and Digest."
• Performance: Reduced Breathing builds CO₂ tolerance, delaying breathlessness and improving endurance.
• Focus: Box Breathing provides "Tactical Calm" to maintain awareness under pressure.
• Deep Sleep: Sleep 4:6 and Humming lower your heart rate to prepare the body for restorative rest.

Tracking Progress (BOLT): The Body Oxygen Level Test measures functional CO₂ tolerance. As your score increases, your energy and stress resilience improve. (Healthy Target: 25s; Elite: 40s).

Practice Tips: Consistency is key—10–20 minutes daily is ideal. Practice should never be a struggle; if you gasp at the end of a round, you are pushing too hard. Never practice holds while driving or in water.
