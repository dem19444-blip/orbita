ORBITA GUIDE

The Bohr Effect: This is the foundation of Oxygen Advantage. It states that for oxygen to be released from your blood into your muscles and brain, you need a certain level of Carbon Dioxide (CO₂) in your blood. By training your body to tolerate higher levels of CO₂, you unlock your body's ability to oxygenate itself more efficiently.

Nasal Breathing: Unless an exercise specifies otherwise, always breathe through the nose. Nasal breathing increases Nitric Oxide production, which is a powerful vasodilator and helps protect the lungs.

Practice Tips:

  • Environment: Sit upright with a straight spine or lie down in a quiet space.
  • Frequency: Aim for 10-20 minutes daily. Consistency is the primary driver of physiological change.
  • Effort: Breathwork should never feel like a struggle. If you feel panic or gasping, you have pushed too far. Return to normal nasal breathing.

The Assessments:

The BOLT Score and Control Pause are tools to measure your progress. A healthy goal is 25 seconds; elite athletic performance often requires 40 seconds. Do not treat these as tests of willpower, but as honest checks of your current chemistry.

Safety: Never practice breath-holding while driving or in water. If you feel lightheaded, distressed, or panicked, stop immediately. Consult a doctor if you have known cardiovascular or respiratory issues.

Goal-Based Recommendations:

• Calm & Recovery: Resonance 5.5, Extended Exhale.
• Instant De-stress: Physiological Sigh (1-2 mins).
• Improve Fitness: BOLT, Reduced Breathing, CP.
• Deep Focus: Box Breathing.
• Onset Sleep: Sleep 4:6, Humming Breath.
